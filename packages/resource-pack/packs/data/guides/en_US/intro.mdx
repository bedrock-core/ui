Some addons in this world are built on **@bedrock-core**. They share the same menus, so what you
learn in one works in all of them.

- **Addons**: every @bedrock-core addon in the world, with its version, its settings and its
  guide. Open the list with `/<addon>:catalog`.
- **Settings**: change how an addon behaves. Everyone can change their own settings, and operators
  can also change them for the whole world. Open them with `/<addon>:config`.
- **Guides**: pages like this one, in your language. Open an addon's guide with `/<addon>:guide`.
