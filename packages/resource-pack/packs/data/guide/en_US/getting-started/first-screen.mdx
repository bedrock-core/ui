# Your First Screen

The guide is two screens you spread into your navigator:

```tsx
...createGuideScreens(staticGuideSource(guides))
```

## Embedding

- Merge `GuideRoutes` into your route map
- Spread `createGuideScreens(...)` into the stack screens
  - The screens plug into any existing navigator
- Navigate to `GuideHome` from anywhere
